## 1. Ethical keywords

**Failures of perception**: moral myopia · moral blindness · bounded ethicality · ethical fading · selective perception · normalization of deviance

**Failures of voice**: moral muteness · organisational silence · groupthink · bystander effect · diffusion of responsibility · pluralistic ignorance

**Internal conflict**: cognitive dissonance · dharma-sankat · ethical dilemma vs ethical lapse · conflict of interest · moral distress · moral injury

**Rationalisation**: moral licensing · slippery slope · banality of evil (Arendt) · euphemistic labelling · displacement of responsibility

**Positive constructs**: moral courage · moral imagination · integrity vs compliance · civic virtue · public service motivation · empathy vs sympathy · compassion fatigue · trusteeship · nishkama karma · sthitaprajna · sarvodaya · antyodaya · ethical audit

**Systemic**: tragedy of the commons · moral hazard · principal-agent problem · regulatory capture · institutional decay · red-tapism · rule of law vs rule by law · letter vs spirit of law · administrative discretion · whistleblowing

## 3. Indian traditions, scripture and mythology

- **Gita**: nishkama karma (action without attachment to fruit) · sthitaprajna (steady wisdom) · svadharma · Arjuna's dilemma as the archetypal ethical conflict
- **Ramayana**: Rama's maryada · Bharata's renunciation of the throne · Vibhishana choosing dharma over kin · Jatayu's duty against impossible odds
- **Mahabharata**: Yudhishthira's half-truth at Drona's death (means-ends) · Karna's loyalty against dharma · Bhishma's oath as rigid rule-following · Vidura Niti on counsel to power · Draupadi's questioning of the assembly's silence (moral muteness)
- **Buddhism**: Eightfold Path · Middle Path · karuna and metta · Ashoka's Dhamma edicts as ethical statecraft
- **Jainism**: ahimsa · anekantavada (many-sidedness — excellent for tolerance and dissent questions) · aparigraha (non-possession)
- **Sikhism**: seva · sarbat da bhala · kirat karo, naam japo, vand chhako
- **Islam**: zakat · ihsan · amanah (trust in office)
- **Christianity**: Good Samaritan · render unto Caesar
- **Kabir and Rahim**: dohas on humility, hypocrisy, speech and restraint — quote in the original with a one-line meaning
- **Tamil / Sangam**: Thirukkural couplets on justice and rulership

## 4. Constitutional and governance anchors

Preamble values (justice, liberty, equality, fraternity) · constitutional morality · rule of law · Article 14 non-arbitrariness · Article 21 dignity · Fundamental Duties (Art 51A) · Directive Principles · oath of office · public trust doctrine · separation of powers · judicial review.

Governance: 2nd ARC reports (esp. *Ethics in Governance*) · Nolan Committee · Santhanam Committee · Code of Conduct vs Code of Ethics · Citizen's Charter · RTI Act · Whistle Blowers Protection Act · Lokpal and Lokayuktas · Prevention of Corruption Act · e-governance and transparency · Sevottam · social audit · Mission Karmayogi.

## 5. Example buckets

Use at least four buckets per answer; no bucket more than twice.

**Leaders and public figures**: Gandhi · Ambedkar · Lal Bahadur Shastri · Sardar Patel · Kalam · Mandela · Lincoln · Florence Nightingale · Mother Teresa · Nelson Mandela's reconciliation over retribution

**Mythology and scripture**: see section 3

**Civil servants and public officials**: Satyendra Dubey (NHAI whistleblower) · Manjunath Shanmugam (IOC) · T.N. Seshan (electoral reform) · E. Sreedharan (delivery with integrity) · Armstrong Pame (people's road, Manipur) · Ashok Khemka · U. Sagayam · S.R. Sankaran · Prashant Nair (Compassionate Kozhikode) · Verghese Kurien · M.S. Swaminathan

**Everyday and personal life**: a teacher who stayed after hours · a colleague who owned a mistake nobody would have traced · returning excess change · a family caregiving decision · refusing to pass on an unverified forward. One micro-example of this kind humanises an answer and is the bucket aspirants most often skip.

**Recent current affairs**: draw from the last 12–18 months — administrative reforms, judicial pronouncements on rights and accountability, disaster response, corporate governance failures, AI and data ethics debates, climate commitments. Verify before asserting; flag anything uncertain with `[verify]` rather than risking a wrong fact in a graded answer.

## Quote hygiene

Use quotes the aspirant can reproduce exactly. A half-remembered quote in the exam is worse than none. When supplying a quote, prefer short and certain over long and impressive, and mark `[verify source]` where attribution is doubtful.
