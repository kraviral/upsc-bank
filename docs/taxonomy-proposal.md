# PYQ taxonomy — proposal

Derived from the Mains Microthemes compilation (2013-2025), then cleaned: compound
`A x B` themes resolved to their first element, near-duplicates merged, thin themes
folded into siblings, and misfiled questions relocated.

**974 questions · 132 themes · 23 topics · avg 7.4 questions per theme**

Mark up anything you want renamed, split, merged or moved. Nothing is filed against
this until you approve it.
